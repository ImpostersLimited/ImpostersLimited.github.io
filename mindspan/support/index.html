MindSpan Support

MindSpan turns your study material into recall cards and schedules reviews to help you remember it.

Importing material

Open Study, choose an import option, and select the material you want to study. Card generation runs on your device. Keep MindSpan open while a large import is being processed.

Updating an import

Choose an existing source when importing again to refresh its generated cards. Cards you have already reviewed or edited are preserved where possible.

Purchases and restores

Purchases are processed by Apple. To restore paid access, open Settings in MindSpan and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Study reminders

Reminder permission can be changed in iOS Settings under Notifications. MindSpan schedules reminders only after you grant permission.

Data and privacy

You can delete sources, cards, and imported media from within the app. Read the MindSpan Privacy Policy for more information.

Contact

For help, email enquiry@imposters.biz. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include private study material.
