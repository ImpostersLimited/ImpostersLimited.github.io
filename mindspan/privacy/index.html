MindSpan Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

MindSpan is designed to process your study material on your device. This policy explains what the app stores and how Apple services may process limited information when you enable their features.

Study data

MindSpan stores imported material, generated cards, source excerpts, study history, notes, guides, and preferences on your device. Card generation uses Apple's on-device language model when available or a model bundled with the app. This card-generation workflow does not send your study content to a third-party AI service.

Study guides and Private Cloud Compute

On supported devices, requesting a study guide may send selected card questions, answers, and source excerpts to Apple's Private Cloud Compute for processing. If that service is unavailable or cannot complete the request, MindSpan tries on-device generation. Apple provides the cloud processing under its Private Cloud Compute protections. MindSpan does not operate an AI server or sell this content.

Study backups

When you export a study backup, the file contains your study records and preferences in a readable format. Store it only in a location you trust. Original imported files are not included. Sharing or saving that backup outside the app is your choice.

iCloud

When iCloud is available, MindSpan may sync app data and imported media through your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. Apple processes this data under your Apple account and its privacy policy. MindSpan does not use a public database to share your study content with other users.

Purchases

Apple processes MindSpan subscriptions and lifetime purchases. The app reads purchase and entitlement status from Apple's StoreKit services so it can unlock paid access and restore purchases. MindSpan does not receive your payment-card details.

Notifications

If you enable study reminders, MindSpan schedules notifications on your device. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Tracking and analytics

MindSpan does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, social login, or a third-party crash-reporting service. MindSpan does not sell personal data.

Deletion

You can delete sources, cards, and imported media in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data. Data synchronized through iCloud may remain in your iCloud account until it is removed through the app or your Apple account. Apple retains purchase records as required to provide and restore purchases and to meet legal obligations.
