Basketball Franchise GM Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

About this policy

Basketball Franchise GM is an offline basketball franchise simulation from Imposters Studio. This policy covers the app.

Data from the app

The app does not transmit your career, gameplay, roster, or settings to us. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, or tracking. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Local storage and retention

Your career and preferences are stored on your device. They remain until you reset the career or delete the app. Device backups may have separate retention under your Apple settings. We do not operate a cloud-sync service for this app.

Support you choose to request

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to and resolve your request. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence by emailing the address below.

Apple services and external links

App Store purchases and Apple diagnostic services are governed by Apple’s privacy settings and policies. Links you open in your browser are governed by the destination website’s privacy practices; website hosts may receive ordinary web-request information. Browsing those pages is not required to play the game.

Changes

We will update this policy and its date when the app’s privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email enquiry@imposters.biz.
