Basketball Franchise GM Support

Need help with your franchise? Contact Imposters Studio at enquiry@imposters.biz.

Report a problem

Include the app version, iOS version, the screen you were using, and the steps that led to the issue. You choose which information and screenshots to send; the app does not automatically upload your saved career.

Saved careers

Careers are stored on your device. If a career cannot be loaded, keep the app installed and avoid replacing the career while contacting support.

Purchases

The app is paid upfront. It has no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Purchase receipts and refund requests are handled by Apple.
