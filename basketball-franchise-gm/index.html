Basketball Franchise GM

Build a basketball franchise, one season at a time.

Run your team

Manage a fictional eight-team league with a compact 14-game regular season and single-game playoffs. Sign free agents, make trades, set rotations, scout prospects, and build toward a championship.

Manage the action

Follow live 2D games, adjust tactics, make legal substitutions, and call timeouts. Review box scores and standings, or simulate games to focus on franchise decisions.

One purchase, offline play

No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Your career is saved on your device.

Basketball Franchise GM uses fictional teams and players and is not affiliated with a professional basketball league.
